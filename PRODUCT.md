# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese-speaking language learners use asbplayer while watching subtitled media, organizing shows into personal anime spaces, and reviewing AI-assisted subtitle analysis. The service is invitation-based, with administrators responsible for accounts and access.

## Product Purpose

The product keeps media playback local while giving each signed-in learner a persistent archive of shows, episodes, and subtitle analysis. It also gives the community a transparent view of member activity without exposing API keys, passwords, media files, or private analysis content.

## Brand Personality

Focused, trustworthy, and calm. The interface should feel like a capable media tool: familiar enough to use immediately, precise about permissions, and quiet around the content.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-dashboard decoration, competitive leaderboards, excessive card grids, ornamental gradients, and admin controls that hide the consequence of an action. Never expose secrets, password material, media filenames, or subtitle analysis in public member views.

## Design Principles

- Keep playback and study workflows primary; account management stays easy to reach but out of the way.
- Make ownership and permission boundaries explicit before any sensitive action.
- Show useful community activity as neutral facts, not rankings or engagement pressure.
- Prefer familiar controls, direct labels, and reversible account operations.
- Preserve local-media privacy while making server-stored metadata understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. All controls require visible focus states, descriptive labels, and non-color status cues. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep dense account tables usable on narrow screens through structural responsive layouts.
